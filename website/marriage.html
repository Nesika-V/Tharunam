Stage Decor

Set the perfect stage for your 'I Do's.' Our Silver package features a beautifully themed floral mandap and stage setup. We combine elegant drapery, seasonal blooms, and professional spotlighting to create a sophisticated focal point. Whether you prefer a traditional Vedic setup or a modern minimalist aesthetic, our designers craft a backdrop that makes every moment look like a masterpiece.

Photography & Videography

Preserve your heritage with high-definition memories. Our professional team provides full-event coverage, including traditional photography and cinematic video highlights. We focus on capturing the raw emotions—the laughter, the sacred rituals, and the candid glances. You will receive a professionally edited digital gallery and a highlight film that lets you relive your wedding day for years to come.

Makeup & Styling

Look your absolute best on your big day with our premium makeup and styling services. Our professional makeup artists specialize in elegant event looks, ensuring you appear confident and camera-ready throughout the celebration. From subtle natural styles to glamorous event makeup, we provide personalized styling that complements your personality and event theme.

Catering Services

Treat your guests to a rich culinary journey. Our Silver catering menu features a curated selection of traditional and contemporary dishes, prepared with the freshest ingredients. We provide a full service team, elegant buffet setups, and a diverse menu (Welcome Drinks, Starters, Main Course, and Desserts) designed to delight every palate.

Grand Couple Entry

Make an entrance that no one will forget. We coordinate a choreographed grand entry for the couple, featuring traditional elements like Nadaswaram or modern touches like cold-fire pyros and flower petal showers. It's the perfect way to announce your arrival and kick off the festivities with energy and style.

Designer Invitations

The celebration begins the moment your guests receive their invite. Our Silver package includes beautifully crafted physical invitation cards or premium digital E-invites. Customized Theme Decor

The decoration includes elegant floral arrangements, luxury fabric draping, decorative lighting elements, and artistic stage structures that match the selected theme. Every design element is carefully arranged to create a cohesive and visually stunning wedding environment.

Full Mandap Decoration

Unlike standard wedding packages that focus only on the stage, the Platinum package decorates the entire mandap area with premium floral arrangements, decorative pillars, hanging flower installations, and ambient lighting. This creates a sacred and majestic atmosphere for the wedding rituals while providing an extraordinary visual experience for guests and photographers.

Welcome Area & Photo Booth

The entrance of the wedding venue is beautifully decorated with floral arches, welcome boards, and elegant lighting elements that create a grand first impression for arriving guests. Live Traditional Music

To enhance the traditional atmosphere of the wedding ceremony, the Platinum package includes a live Nathasvaram performance or a vibrant Chenda Melam drum ensemble. These traditional musical performances create a festive and culturally rich environment that adds grandeur to the rituals and the couple's entrance moments.

Photography, Makeup & Catering
